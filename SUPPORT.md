# Support

## Getting Help

If you need help installing, using, or building **HealthyCropping**, please check these resources first:

1. **Documentation**:
   - [`README.md`](README.md) — Overview, requirements, and quickstart.
   - [`docs/SETUP_SERVICES_ES.md`](docs/SETUP_SERVICES_ES.md) — Step-by-step guide (in Spanish) for configuring Firebase, Gemini, Cloudinary, Render, Kaggle, Colab, and building/installing the APK.
   - [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) — Technical architecture and module breakdown.
   - [`docs/MODEL_CARD.md`](docs/MODEL_CARD.md) — Model capabilities, supported classes, confidence thresholds, and limitations.

2. **GitHub Issues**:
   - Search [existing issues](https://github.com/keylorpineda/healthycrop/issues) to see if your question or problem has already been reported.
   - Found a bug? Open a [**Bug Report**](https://github.com/keylorpineda/healthycrop/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
   - Found an inaccurate crop disease prediction? Open a [**Model Prediction Issue**](https://github.com/keylorpineda/healthycrop/issues/new?template=model_issue.yml).
   - Want to propose an improvement? Open a [**Feature Request**](https://github.com/keylorpineda/healthycrop/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

## Agronomic Disclaimer

**HealthyCropping is an open-source, non-commercial decision-support tool and does not replace a certified agronomist or phytosanitary laboratory.** Disease predictions depend on image quality, lighting, plant part framing, and model coverage. Before applying chemical treatments, pruning, or removing plants, consult a qualified agricultural professional or local extension office (such as ICAFE or MAG in Costa Rica).
